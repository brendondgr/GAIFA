Brightfield K7 headlamp: specifications

Light1,600 lm max, 6 modes, red light
BatteryOne 21700 Li-ion cell, 5000 mAh (included)
Other batteries18650 cells fit with the BF-S18 sleeve adapter (sold separately). Update the lamp to firmware 1.4 or later first, so it uses the right low-voltage cutoff for an 18650 cell. AA and AAA batteries don't fit.
ChargingUSB-C, 2 A, about 3.5 hours from empty
Weight148 g with the 21700 cell

Updated 30 June 2026: the BF-S18 adapter is now available; earlier K7 lamps take it after the firmware update.
