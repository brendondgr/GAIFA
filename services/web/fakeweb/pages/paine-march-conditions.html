Torres del Paine in March: trail conditions and gear

Notes from our guides' 2026 season on the W trek and the O circuit, updated 8 April 2026.

What March was like

March is late summer turning to autumn: fewer people, shorter days and more rain than January. In March 2026 it rained on 17 of 31 days, and the Valle Francés and the Los Perros forest sections had ankle-deep mud on 9 of 14 days our guides walked them. Wind on the Paso John Gardner pass reached 95 km/h, and fresh snow lay down to 1,180 m in the last week of the month.

Boots

On 8-day trips our guides now recommend a synthetic boot with a waterproof membrane: it dries overnight in a refugio drying room, where a soaked full-grain leather boot was still wet the next morning on most of our trips. Leather lasts longer and keeps its shape on the scree below the towers, so it suits you if you already own well broken-in leather boots and wax them before the trip. Either way, don't start in new boots.

Gaiters

Bring gaiters in March. Low ankle gaiters are enough on the W; on the O circuit take knee-high gaiters for the mud between Campamento Los Perros and Paso John Gardner, and for the snow on the pass late in the month.
