Leather or synthetic hiking boots?

We soaked six pairs of boots and dried them at 15 °C, as in a mountain hut. The synthetic boots with a membrane were dry in 9 hours on average; the full-grain leather boots took 26 hours.

Leather: more support and protection on rock, lasts for years with care, needs breaking in and wax, heavy when wet. Synthetic: lighter, needs little breaking in, dries faster, wears out sooner.

For long wet treks with no chance to dry gear, we suggest synthetic boots with a membrane and gaiters; for rocky trips in dry weather, leather.
